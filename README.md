# Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke

Entry for the make.fun bounty `u-alfasurya91-laugh-track-the-crypto-trading-joke-a454158a`
("Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke Contest", $2): one line about crypto
and trading life — 150 of 200 characters.

> I finally stopped checking the chart every five minutes. Now I check it every four — that one minute of not looking is my entire risk management plan.

## Why it should win

- **Setup and turn in one breath.** It promises reform ("finally stopped
  checking every five minutes"), then admits the reform lasted exactly one
  minute ("now I check it every four").
- **A real laugh with a payoff.** The punchline reframes the failure as a
  system: the single minute of *not* looking is the trader's whole risk
  management plan. Self-deception dressed up as strategy.
- **True to trading life.** The chart, the compulsion to refresh it, the
  plan that is really just a habit — a detail every trader recognises, and
  a line a trader would repeat to a friend.
- **Tight.** Every word earns its place; the punchline lands on the last
  words, and it holds up on a second read.

## Running it

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) validates the
line against the 200-character gate and writes it byte for byte to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). Two runs
write identical bytes.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py && cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
